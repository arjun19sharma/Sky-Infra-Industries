Privacy Policy

We believe communication should be built on trust. Here's a straightforward explanation of how privacy is approached when you visit or contact Sky Infra Industries.

● Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Overview

Sky Infra Industries respects your privacy. This website is primarily an informational website intended to introduce our business, products and contact details.

We do not provide visitor accounts or use a website enquiry form to collect personal information. We do not intentionally collect personal details simply because you visit or browse this website.

2. Information you choose to share

If you contact us by email, telephone or WhatsApp, you may choose to share information such as your name, contact details, business details or enquiry requirements.

We use information you voluntarily share only as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, communicate with you, discuss your requirements or follow up on the conversation.

Please avoid sharing passwords, financial account credentials or other sensitive information through ordinary messages.

3. Email, WhatsApp and external services

When you select an email link, your device may open your chosen email application. When you select a WhatsApp contact link, you may be redirected to WhatsApp.

These services are operated by third parties and are subject to their own privacy policies, terms and practices. Information you send through them is handled by the relevant service as well as by the recipient.

We do not control how third-party platforms process information. Please review their policies before using them.

4. Cookies and technical information

We do not intentionally use this website to request personal information through cookies or visitor tracking forms.

However, the website's hosting provider, server infrastructure or security services may automatically process limited technical information, such as IP addresses, browser details, access times and server logs, for website delivery, security and troubleshooting.

If any third-party features or analytics are added in the future, this policy may be updated to explain them.

5. How we use information

Information you voluntarily provide may be used for legitimate business communication, including:

  • Responding to your questions and enquiries.
  • Discussing products, pricing or material requirements.
  • Communicating about a potential or existing business interaction.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations where required.

We do not intend to sell your personal information or use it for unrelated purposes.

6. Security and retention

We take reasonable care when handling information shared with us and aim to avoid unauthorised access, disclosure, misuse or loss.

No method of online communication or electronic storage is completely secure. Information shared through email, messaging services or other third-party platforms may also be subject to their security practices.

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was shared, or where retention is required by applicable law.

7. Your choices

You are free to browse this website without submitting an enquiry. Contacting us and sharing personal information is your choice.

If you have shared information with us and would like to request its correction or deletion, you can contact us. We will consider your request subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate record-keeping needs.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, our practices or applicable legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact us

If you have a question or concern about this Privacy Policy or information you have shared with us, please contact:
