WHO WE ARE

A Business Built Around People, Quality & Trust.

Sky Infra Industries is a construction material supply company focused on supporting the requirements of the construction and infrastructure sector. Our areas of specialisation include PVC, HDPE, Steel and several other types of pipes, along with a wider range of construction materials and essential raw materials.

We understand that every project has its own requirements, priorities and expectations. Choosing the right materials and the right supply partner can make a meaningful difference to the overall experience of a project.

That is why we believe in understanding our customers' needs, focusing on quality-oriented sourcing and offering competitive pricing. Our objective is to make material procurement a more dependable and straightforward experience for the businesses and professionals we serve.

Above all, we are here to build relationships that extend beyond individual orders. We value every opportunity to work with our customers and aim to become a trusted partner they can return to.

"Nothing is more important than the relationships we build and the trust we earn."
